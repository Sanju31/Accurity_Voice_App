How to use this lab

  1. Select a prospect persona and click Start roleplay.

    Call flow cheat sheet

    Capture attention

    Ask permission for 30 seconds. Identify Accurity and ask whether the prospect is already ordering UAD 3.6 or preparing for the transition.

    Find the business need
    • Which counties or markets matter most?
    • Where do you need extra capacity or coverage?
    • What turnaround and revision performance do you expect?
    Connect capability to need

    Explain production experience, USPAP-compliant reporting, staff-appraiser coordination, coverage assessment, and capacity. Only share approved metrics.

    Ask for a specific next step

    Request a 15-minute discussion. If there is a fit, propose three to five paid trial assignments subject to approved coverage, pricing, and capacity.

    Listen → acknowledge → ask a relevant question

    “We already have vendors.” → “That makes sense. Would an additional approved option for overflow or limited-coverage counties be useful?”

    “Send information.” → “Of course. Would production experience, county coverage, or pricing and turnaround be most useful?”

    “What is your turnaround?” → “It depends on county, complexity, and capacity. We’ll confirm the available turnaround before committing.”
